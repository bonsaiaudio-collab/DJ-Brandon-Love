Working career chronology

This is an evidence-led timeline, not a claim that every project was active continuously. Dates are approximate or qualified where records are incomplete.

2009–2011 · Convention-center audiovisual work (resume-reported)

A retrospective resume lists Brandon Andersen as an audiovisual technician at Three Rivers Convention Center from September 2009 to September 2011. This date range has not yet been independently corroborated.

Source: user-supplied resume; verification pending

2011 · DJ BounceHouse and Metropolis

A user-supplied original flyer advertises “BounceHouse Presents: Metropolis” for August 5, 2011 at Club Metropoly in Pasco, Washington. Historical email and video traces support an active DJ BounceHouse identity in this period. The flyer establishes promotion, not independently confirmed attendance or performances.

Sources: original promotional flyer; contemporaneous 2011 email and video notifications

Early years · Mixed Media Services and Boogie Maximus

Andersen identifies Mixed Media Services as his first business name and recalls that the DJ identity Boogie Maximus replaced DJ BounceHouse. Exact dates remain unverified.

Source: firsthand recollection, November 2026

2014 · Bonsai Audio founding (recalled)

Andersen has corrected earlier resume claims of a 2010 start date, recalling that Bonsai Audio began in 2014. Independent founding records are still being sought; earlier work should not automatically be attributed to Bonsai Audio.

Source: firsthand correction, November 2026; conflicting older resume dates retained in research records

2019 · Podcast production coordination

Source: original correspondence, July 2019; not publicly reproduced

2020 · Podcast hosting and migration

Source: original correspondence, February 2020

2020 · Bonsai Audio service and studio concepts

Committee materials from 2020 and 2021 document Andersen's participation in community communications and regional project discussions. Specific responsibilities and outcomes should be checked against individual meeting records.
