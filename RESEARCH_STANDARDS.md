# Brandon Andersen professional archive — research standards

Mission: Build the most complete, accurate, evidence-backed public record of my professional life possible.

This is a historical documentation project, not a sales or monetization initiative.

## Source hierarchy
1. Contemporaneous public records, reporting, event programs and archived sites.
2. Contemporaneous original business documents and correspondence, cited descriptively without exposing private information.
3. Later interviews and firsthand recollections, clearly attributed and dated.
4. Unverified claims and leads, excluded from factual public claims until supported.

## Attribution
Differentiate founder, owner, organizer, producer, contracted vendor, collaborator, performer, participant, candidate, and proposer. Never attribute an organization's work wholesale to one person.

## Historical status
Mark projects as proposed, planned, piloted, launched, completed, paused, or unverified, based on evidence. Do not assume a current role from old records.

## Privacy
Do not publish private email bodies, personal contact information, client details, contracts, account identifiers, or sensitive personal circumstances without explicit authorization. Summarize private evidence conservatively.
