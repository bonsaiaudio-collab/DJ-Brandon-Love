Bonsai Events: surviving production records

The following inventory is based on original project folders and documents. Folder names identify intended event dates; a dated folder or poster alone is not proof that a show occurred as scheduled. Bonsai Events is distinct from the DJ/AV business Bonsai Audio.

February 22, 2019 — Entombed: An EDM Revenge

The surviving materials include an event description, artist lineup, promotional artwork, audio assets, ticket-tracking documents and checklists. The story uses an Egyptian pharaoh and a supernatural revenge narrative to frame the performance. A lineup document lists Mobbin' Hud, Ricochet, F3lon, Kalibr and Triggabyte. Promotional copy assigns performance slots from 9 p.m. to 2 a.m. These records document substantial preparation; Contemporaneous January 2019 correspondence describes the show as scheduled for the Uptown Theatre, and February 22 printing correspondence confirms production badges were ready that day. A February 22, 2019 Brown Paper Tickets report identifies the event as “Entombed with Triggabyte” in Richland at 8:30 p.m., recording three advance sales on that platform. This is ticketing evidence, not a total attendance figure or proof of the show's completion. Final execution and attendance remain open.

Original evidence: Entombed Description · Entombed Lineup · project folder. Access to original files may be restricted.

A document titled “Nukid Post Financials” also survives, but its contents have not yet been evaluated and no financial details are published here.

Organizer and creative direction

In a January 24, 2019 outreach email, Andersen described himself as owner of Bonsai Audio and Bonsai Events and stated that he organized recurring EDM events at the Uptown Theatre, working with local businesses and live visual artists. This is a dated first-person statement. May 2019 Nukid correspondence with an artist-management representative further documents Andersen's active involvement in staging, thematic design and production constraints. These emails are cited descriptively but not reproduced publicly.

Sources: original professional correspondence, January 24, February 22, May 22–23 and July 19–24, 2019; Brown Paper Tickets sales report dated February 22, 2019.

Editorial standards

Names and dates above are transcribed from surviving records. They do not imply all listed performers appeared, all planned features were delivered, or that Andersen personally performed every production role. Future revisions will distinguish verified execution from planning and clarify individual credits.
