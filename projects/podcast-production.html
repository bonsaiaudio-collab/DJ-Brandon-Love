Podcast production and publishing

Historical project record · 2019–2020 · Evidence: dated private client correspondence

Tri-Cities Influencer Podcast with Paul Casey

A July 2019 email exchange between Brandon Andersen and host Paul Casey documents Andersen's coordination of episode publication and supporting-content timing. Andersen reported that an episode had been posted, and that upcoming episodes were sent to a production collaborator named James. This supports production coordination and publishing involvement; it does not establish that Andersen hosted the podcast or independently produced every episode.

Just Talk to Jonas

February 2020 emails from Andersen to a project collaborator document episode-hosting arrangements, delivery of podcast links, and migration of hosted episodes to another service to avoid recurring costs. The records support responsibility for technical publishing and hosting logistics, not ownership of the show.

Evidence and limits

Primary evidence consists of original emails dated July 19–20, 2019 and February 4 and 19, 2020. These private records are retained for research but are not reproduced publicly to protect correspondents' privacy. Exact episode counts, contract terms and public credits remain unverified.
