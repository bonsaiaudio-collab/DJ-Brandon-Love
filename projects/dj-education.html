DJ education and wedding-planning writing

Historical drafts · Dates not independently established

How to start a DJ business

An original working document titled “How to start a DJ business” explains why equipment purchases should not be a beginner's first priority. It discusses music literacy, understanding dance styles and audience response, volunteering or shadowing established DJs, and practical performance techniques. The draft includes Andersen's first-person account of paying people who shadowed his DJ work; that statement has not been independently verified.

5 Resources You MUST HAVE While You Plan Your Tri-Cities Wedding

A separate original draft introduces wedding-planning resources, including inspiration boards, planning websites, professional planners and local vendor directories. It demonstrates an effort to create informational material for wedding clients and to refer readers to complementary service providers.

Evidence and limits

Evidence: original document drafts retained in the research collection. Neither draft alone proves public publication, distribution, formal instruction, course enrollment or training outcomes. Publication status and dates remain open research questions.
