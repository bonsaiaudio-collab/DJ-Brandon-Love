# Public wedding testimonials — evidence inventory

Research date: 2026-11-09 (UTC). Source: [DJ Brandon Love on The Knot](https://www.theknot.com/marketplace/dj-brandon-love-kennewick-wa-2102303).

## Evidence classification
Publicly posted customer testimonials, not independent reporting. The listing displayed six reviews and a five-star aggregate when inspected. Counts and ratings can change. No personal names, dates of private weddings, contact details, or full review text are reproduced here.

## Publicly described service experiences
- One customer described the DJ continuing music during rainfall and relocating the party indoors.
- Another customer described planning preparation, music curation across pop, hip-hop and EDM, dance-floor management, and use of silent-disco headphones.
- Another customer described wedding DJ/MC service and advance venue preparation.
- Another customer described wedding transitions and closing the evening.

These are evidence of customers' reported experiences, not independently authenticated proof of every detail. They must not be assigned to historical contract IDs without a direct, reliable match. The review dates are not necessarily event dates.

## Privacy and verification rule
Do not search for or expose private couples' identities merely to match the contract register. Use publicly credited vendor features, organizer acknowledgments, or opt-in testimonials when available. Avoid indexing personal wedding websites, registries, addresses, and guest information.
