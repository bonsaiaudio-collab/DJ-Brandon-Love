Verification policy

A filename is a research lead, not proof of a booking. A signed contract proves an agreement, not delivery. Completion requires separate evidence, such as event photographs, post-event correspondence, payment documentation, or independent coverage. No private contracts, signatures, personal contact details, or financial terms are published.
