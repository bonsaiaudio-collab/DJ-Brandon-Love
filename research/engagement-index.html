Private-event documentation

Additional scanned wedding and private-event PDFs from 2016–2018 have been located. To protect individuals, their names and dates are not published from filenames alone. They will be counted only after deduplication and evidence review.

Verification policy

A filename is a research lead, not proof of a booking. A planning worksheet records a proposed event, not a confirmed contract. Expected guest counts are projections, not actual attendance. A signed contract proves an agreement, not delivery. Completion requires separate evidence, such as event photographs, post-event correspondence, payment documentation, or independent coverage. No private contracts, signatures, personal contact details, or financial terms are published.
