## Research-method pivot

Stop treating historical invoices as likely web-searchable performance credits. Instead, prioritize (1) published business profiles and organizational records; (2) first-party photo/video assets with date, venue and recognizable equipment or signage, documented as owner-supplied evidence; (3) public reviews explicitly identifying the provider; (4) event programs or press coverage that independently name the company. Keep all privacy and event-completion distinctions intact.
