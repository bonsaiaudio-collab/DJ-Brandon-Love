Historical contract evidence

Private contracts are used to substantiate dates, business identity, and scope of work, not published as unredacted documents. A signed agreement establishes a booking, not necessarily completed performance or payment.

2018 — Bonsai Audio community dance engagement

A six-page scanned Bonsai Audio packet documents an agreement signed January 23, 2018, for DJ and event services at a father–daughter dance scheduled February 23, 2018, in Kennewick, Washington. The packet contains a service schedule, music-preference worksheet, MC instructions, contract terms, and signatures. It identifies Bonsai Audio, LLC and Brandon Andersen as its representative. This evidence establishes a signed service agreement and planning process; actual performance and payment are not independently confirmed.

Source type: privately held scanned contract, six pages. Public access: withheld to protect client contact information, signatures, and commercial terms. Verification: visual inspection of original pages.

Standards

Private source records are not external validation. Future entries should distinguish draft worksheets, signed contracts, proof of service, receipts, and independent public corroboration. Do not publish private client details or infer that scheduled events were completed.
