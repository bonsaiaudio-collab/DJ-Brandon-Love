## Research implication
The documents reveal an interdisciplinary approach involving video, costume, staging, fabrication, dancers, sponsorship and local retail distribution. Public claims about actual collaborators require a delivered asset, contemporaneous confirmation, payment, or post-event record. Do not publish private contact details or treat tentative names as confirmed partners.
