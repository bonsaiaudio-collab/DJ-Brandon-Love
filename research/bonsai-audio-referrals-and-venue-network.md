# Bonsai Audio — referrals, venues and business-network evidence

Research date: 2026-11-09 UTC. Sources: connected Gmail; original private correspondence and client records. **This is a redacted research summary, not an endorsement by named businesses.** Do not publish private customer names, contact details, event addresses or budgets.

## Referral system — confirmed
- Exact Gmail subject search `"Brandon - You have received a new referral in BNI Connect!"` yielded **65 matching email messages**. These are **automated referral notifications**, not 65 verified jobs, distinct customers, payments, or DJ bookings. A reviewed Sept 23 2021 message identifies a BNI member referral, but its subject matter concerned a boutique table-zen-garden sale, illustrating that not all referrals were for Bonsai Audio entertainment services.
- A broader search `"BNI Connect" "referral" before:2023/01/01` returned **121 messages**. It may contain duplicates, sent/received notifications, system correspondence and unrelated referrals; do not interpret as a referral count.
- A 2019 website information request states the source as `Referral from several people and business`. Another 2019 venue-associated inquiry lists `Referral`. These are self-reported sources of inbound interest, not verified resulting sales.
- A 2019 inquiry marks `Friend referral`; a 2020 inquiry names that prior wedding couple as the source of referral and describes a past DJ performance. This is a documented referral chain, but the later inquiry's booking outcome is unverified.

## Vendor relationship — Luxe Lace Designs
- March 23 2018 email from a wedding planner at **Luxe Lace Designs** says a couple had chosen Bonsai Audio and expresses interest in adding Brandon to an expanding preferred-vendor network.
- Brandon's March 28 reply proposes meeting at Fuse Coworking in Richland. Subsequent correspondence schedules further networking discussion.
- This proves outreach and potential vendor-list consideration; **not confirmed inclusion in a preferred-vendor list or actual referrals from the planner**.

## Venue and organization leads
- The DJ Event Planner `Request For Information` notifications carry an `Event Location (venue)` field and `How did you hear about us?` field, useful for compiling historical venue associations.
- One June 2019 venue-associated inquiry references **Promise Gardens** as the event venue and organizational affiliation; **this is an inquiry, not proof a service was performed there**.
- Another Dec 2019 inquiry references **Flat Top Event Center** as the planned venue; likewise not proof of performance.
- Other inquiry messages reference additional venues. Build venue links only with evidence-level tags (inquiry vs contracted vs performed).

## Referral-platform listing
- November 13 2019 email from **Mi Padrino** says a customer recommended Bonsai Audio to its vendor directory and claims customer leads; the directory's statements are **marketing claims**, not independent confirmation of bookings or volume.

## Research interpretation
Bonsai Audio had documented customer referrals, direct website lead attribution, wedding-vendor networking and BNI referral-platform activity. A referral notification is not necessarily an AV/DJ/photo-booth job. Do not claim that specific venues endorsed or preferred Bonsai Audio without proof.

## Follow-up
