# Research framework: Bonsai Audio and Bonsai Events

## Distinct historical tracks
**Bonsai Audio**: service operations, including DJ-only, photo-booth-only, AV equipment rental, live sound, lighting, combined service packages, and other documented service lines. Never exclude a paid or contracted photo-booth-only event from the service register because it lacks a DJ.

**Bonsai Events**: events produced or promoted, artist and DJ relationships, venues, co-promoters, sponsors, production crew, organizers, and community gatherings. The goal is an event-by-event record of what occurred, not just what was planned.

These are brand/project distinctions, not an assertion of separate legal entities.

## Minimum data model
- Each event/engagement: canonical event ID, event date or date range, brand(s), service category (DJ / photo booth / AV / lighting / mixed / event production / other), venue or organization where public and appropriate, source references, and status.
- Status must distinguish: proposed; inquiry; advertised; booked/contracted; ticketed; postponed/cancelled; performed/completed; independently corroborated.
- Roles: owner, DJ, promoter, producer, AV provider, photo-booth operator, collaborator, etc., only as supported by source evidence.
- Keep source provenance and evidence confidence separate from event status.
- Protect private wedding clients, personal contacts, financial negotiation, and unpublished attendee images.

## Bonsai Audio research priorities
1. Audit historical contract register for non-DJ services (especially photo-booth-only and AV-only). Earlier DJ-focused attribution of 75 contracted events is not a count of **all Bonsai Audio engagements**.
2. Search connected Gmail for website forms across **all services**, not only the 60 original DJ-services submissions already identified.
3. Look for photo-booth gallery deliveries, rentals, invoices, equipment setup records, and customer acknowledgments.
4. Deduplicate inquiries, reschedules, multiple contracts and follow-ups. Do not call an inquiry a completed booking.

## Bonsai Events research priorities
1. For each named production (Interstellar, Ignition, BLACKMARKET, Nukid, Extinction, After Dark, etc.), establish date, venue, public organizer evidence, lineup, production roles and post-event evidence.
2. Build a **public professional collaboration graph** linking artists, DJs, venues, organizers, sponsors and collaborators to specific productions; verify each relationship individually.
3. Distinguish booking negotiations from signed performance contracts and from completed appearances.
4. Cross-reference owner-held promotional materials and correspondence against independently attributable venue/artist/organizer accounts, contemporary coverage, and archived listings.
5. Do not publish private contact details or assume an artist performed merely because an outreach email or logo asset exists.

## Evidence policy
A source can establish the existence of a document, an inquiry, a negotiation, a contract, or a completed event, depending on what it actually says. Record all, but never silently promote one evidence level to another.
