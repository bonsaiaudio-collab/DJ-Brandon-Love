# Bonsai Audio — first-pass research closeout audit

Reviewed 2026-11-09 UTC. Status: **baseline established; completeness not proven**.

## Historical inquiry channels
- Squarespace `Form Submission - DJ Services Contact Form`: 126 matching Gmail messages before Jan 2022, including 60 exact original-subject messages and 66 replies/variants (prior audit). Not a unique customer or booking count.
- DJ Event Planner: Gmail query `subject:"Request For Information -" before:2023/01/01` returned **204 matching messages**, fully paginated; **181 subject lines begin exactly with `Request For Information - `**. This is a broad subject-pattern count, not deduplicated inquiries. Overlap with Squarespace and other forms is possible. These messages may include venue and lead-source fields.
- A search for `"Photo Booth" "invoice" before:2023/01/01` returned 25 messages, many irrelevant or discussion threads; no defensible photo-booth-only completed-event total from this search.

## Public independent support
- [February 15 2018 SCORE profile](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/articles/1309): reports Bonsai Audio provided **DJ and photo booth services** for weddings, corporate parties, business grand openings and other events; reports five part-time employees at that time, plus mentoring and Chamber networking. The employee count is contemporaneous, not an all-time figure.
- [April 15 2021 pandemic-pivot profile](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/articles/3191): reports DJ/AV and nonprofit event services, COVID interruption, and bonsai tree import and retail pivot including CG Public House drive-through markets.
- [September 2017 business listing](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/articles/1010) and [July 2021 business listing](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/articles/3333): third-party business listings, not evidence of individual events.

## Scope accounted for
DJ services, photo booths (including stand-alone service), AV rentals/sound/lighting, direct website inquiries, DJEP lead forms, venue leads, customer referrals and BNI, bonsai-tree retail. Keep distinct service categories and independent evidence levels.

## Remaining limitations
- No exhaustive photo-booth-only or AV-only engagement reconciliation against all contracts, invoices, delivery records and photos.
- DJEP and Squarespace inquiry totals are message counts and may overlap.
- Venue names in inquiries do not prove Bonsai Audio performed there or was preferred by the venue.
- 46 Squarespace order notifications (2020–22 search) include non-tree items; tree units, refunds and fulfillment not reconciled.
- Referral notifications can concern non-entertainment products; not all represent closed jobs.

## Closeout decision
**Bonsai Audio can be considered first-pass research baseline complete for major business lines, but not transaction-complete or venue-complete.** Preserve a targeted backlog instead of making unsupported completeness claims. Shift primary investigative effort to Bonsai Events' event-by-event independent corroboration and collaborator/venue network. Do not publish private client identities or contact information.
