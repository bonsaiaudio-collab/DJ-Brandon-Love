## Cauldron — especially strong post-event retrospective
[Cauldron Impressions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oyAmesHK2vhfd2ii8q03JGyiOnyLlqEmaZyze3qtphs/edit) was shared by Bonsai Audio on **Oct 23 2018**, four days after scheduled event. It records an internal team retrospective:
- Low turnout, labor, venue-change difficulties, losses on rentals and advertising; the notes describe **Cauldron as an event that was thrown**, not merely planned.
- Team members discuss unveiling a stage design, favorable reactions from some industry contacts, and connections to people in Pullman and Seattle.
- Brandon distinguished **Bonsai Events as community-building, not a straightforward profit center for Bonsai Audio**, and discussed restructuring compensation, volunteers and profit sharing.
- Next planned production was **Crystallize: An EDM Vision**, Dec 21.
- Ideas involving quarterly Uptown events, Bass Therapy collaboration, a street team, sponsorships and a nightclub are **proposals only**, not proof implemented.

**Evidence assessment:** The dated post-event retrospective is strong internal evidence that Cauldron **actually took place**, despite its low turnout and change to Shilo Inn. No independently sourced venue report, audience count or final performer list has been confirmed.
