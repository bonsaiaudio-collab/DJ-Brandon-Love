# Bonsai Events 2018 — venue changes and community partnership evidence

Research date: 2026-11-09 UTC. Primary sources are original Google Drive and connected Gmail records, not independent press coverage. Source emails contain personal contact details; those are deliberately omitted here.

## Radioactive at Uptown — August 17 2018 (archived date)
- [Original project folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1AoCI_F-xqkkFIbuoKyKbtqb7mQyZm2ci) includes `Event Description`, `Line Up`, `Sponsorships and Vendors`, `Event Sponsorship Information`, performer folder, and a July 2018 Tumbleweird advertisement asset. The ad asset is **not yet verified as published**.
- [Original event description](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YA11TNz8pIoFnChMnDnvkmQdiiEN_VD9gv2vKRHrA8o/edit) identifies the historic Uptown Theater as the intended site and describes house, bass, dubstep and trap music, lighting and sound; this is organizer promotional copy, not independently verified performance.
- **August 7–9 2018 two-way email thread**, subject `Booth Sponsorship at Radioactive: an EDM Experience at the Uptown Theater`, documents Bonsai Audio collaborator **Stephanie Waggener** arranging a sponsored information booth with **My Friends Place** (program contact at Safe Harbor). The program representative agreed to bring two staff and requested a table; Stephanie agreed to provide one. This is **strong contemporaneous evidence of a community organization partnership planned for the event**, but not yet evidence the booth was actually operated on event night.
- The correspondence also documents a third-party sponsor underwriting the booth, **but the sponsor is not named** in the inspected passage. Do not identify the sponsor by guess.

## Cauldron — October 19 2018 (archived date)
- [Original project folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1gBZz8aXqg4zFvuW0TuGf6-l1C8vkw2l3) contains event description, set-times image, artist intro graphics, volunteer calls, VIP giveaway graphics, badges, and several **new-venue** poster variants.
- [Venue-change announcement](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gSFr6jvHB8rUAKaLJDRIJNph39w8nlBweD6F2P5pn-Y/edit) and a corresponding **October 10 2018** email notification explicitly state the original **Uptown Theatre** date had been double-booked and **Shilo Inn, Richland** was selected as replacement. These are organizer statements and announcements, not venue-origin confirmation that the event occurred.
- The announcement expresses continued intent to work with Uptown Theatre; do not claim a formal ongoing venue partnership beyond the text.

## Crystalize — December 21 2018 (archived date)
- [Original project folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1YKa84TPlSGsyyxnR0bWZMCYfKLVFQ6ko) includes Bonsai Events-branded artwork, named artist-logo assets, VIP promotions, volunteer planning and an intros folder. Logo assets indicate planned promotion; **do not infer that every artist performed**.

## Significance
The 2018 archive documents an event-production network with venue negotiations, sponsor/booth arrangements, a community organization, volunteers, performer marketing and production collaborators. It predates the better-documented 2019 series.
