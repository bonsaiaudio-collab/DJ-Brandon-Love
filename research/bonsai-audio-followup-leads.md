# Bonsai Audio — expanded public-source sweep

Research date: 2026-11-09 UTC. Public evidence only. No private client details.

## Newly identified lead requiring primary-source confirmation

- [Washington Company Search — Bonsai entries](https://www.washingtoncompanysearch.com/BONSAI/): commercial directory reports BONSAI AUDIO, LLC, UBI 604151208, incorporated July 17, 2017, and an administratively dissolved status. **Provisional third-party claim.** Verify identity, formation date, status, and status history using the Washington Secretary of State official corporations database before publishing as established fact. Do not conflate LLC formation with the start of the owner's DJ career.

## Reconfirmed independently published context

- [Tri-Cities Area Journal of Business, February 2018](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/articles/1309): SCORE profile describes Bonsai Audio as an entertainment company offering DJ and photo-booth services and reports five part-time employees at the time. This is a historical staffing claim, not a current count.
- [Tri-Cities Area Journal of Business, April 2021](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/articles/3191): reports event-business pivot during pandemic and the owner's recollection of typical summer wedding volume. Booking volume is an interview claim, not independently audited performance count.
- [Tri-City Herald, July 2021](https://www.tri-cityherald.com/news/politics-government/election/article252924408.html): identifies owner of Bonsai Audio and reports civic volunteer roles and business pivot.
- [Tri-City Regional Chamber, March 2018](https://www.tricityregionalchamber.com/uploads/4/8/9/6/48961239/march_2018_newsletter.pdf): contemporaneous membership listing. The printed surname spelling differs from the owner's; note this without treating it as a different person.
- [DexKnows Pasco event-planners directory](https://www.dexknows.com/pasco-wa/party-event-planners): currently indexed category listing for Bonsai Audio as party planning, audiovisual equipment rentals, and DJs. Undated directory metadata is not proof of historical dates or present operation.
