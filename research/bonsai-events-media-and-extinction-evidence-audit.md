## Attribution boundaries
Promotional artwork is evidence of design and preparation, not event execution. Folder labels alone cannot identify photographers, videographers, designers, dancers, or paid collaborators. Future audit should inspect video files, image metadata and original social posts, with consent-aware treatment of identifiable individuals.
