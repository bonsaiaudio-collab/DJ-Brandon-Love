## Crystallize: An EDM Vision — December 21, 2018
**Unsolicited firsthand attendee email**, dated **Dec 29 2018**, subject `Booking`, from an aspiring Richland DJ: the correspondent says they attended Crystallize and describes it as an impressive show, asking about future performance opportunities. This is **firsthand non-organizer testimony of event occurrence**, though preserved in a private email rather than a publicly accessible review. Do not publish correspondent identity or email without permission.
- Brandon replied Dec 29 with an invitation to join a promo team, describing a proposed development pathway for local DJs, practice and eventual bookings. He reported DJs associated with the events were beginning to play in Seattle and Portland; this is **his contemporaneous claim, not independently verified individual career outcomes**.
- Jan 2019 follow-up correspondence from Stephanie confirms a promo-team meeting was being organized, not that any specific correspondent ultimately performed.
