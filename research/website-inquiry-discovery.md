# Historical Bonsai Audio website inquiries — Gmail discovery

Research date: 2026-11-09 UTC. **Private-source inventory only.** Do not reproduce individual inquiries, names, email addresses, phone numbers, event addresses or budgets publicly.

## Verified email pattern
- Gmail exact-subject search `subject:"Form Submission - DJ Services Contact Form" before:2022/01/01` returned **126 matching messages**, fully paginated at research time.
- **60 messages** have the exact original subject `Form Submission - DJ Services Contact Form`; **66** are replies or other subject variants within that query. These are **message counts, not unique people, unique inquiries, paid bookings or completed events**.
- A sampled original dated December 29, 2021 explicitly states that it was sent via a form on **bonsaiaudio.com** and includes fields for requested services, budget and event details. This confirms the email pattern as an inbound website form.
- These records are distinct from The Knot marketplace leads and DJ Event Planner software newsletters.
