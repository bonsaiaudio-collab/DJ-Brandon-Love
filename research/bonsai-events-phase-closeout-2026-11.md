# Bonsai Events historical research — phase closeout (November 2026)

## Purpose and scope
Evidence-backed documentation of the Bonsai Events production work in 2018–2019 using surviving Bonsai Audio account records, Drive production documents, transactional receipts and original media. Distinguish Bonsai Events from Bonsai Audio DJ/AV services. This is a **research-phase closeout**, not a claim that every archival source or independent public record has been exhausted.

## Strongest documented achievements
1. **Radioactive — Aug 17, 2018.** Day-of Square report recorded 141 paid admission items and $2,134 account-wide gross that date, with item-to-gross reconciliation still incomplete. Brown Paper Tickets records and a detailed staffing sheet survive. A Sept 5, 2018 contemporary email shared event photos and a Radioactive recap link. The linked file currently returns not found from the connected account. The sales data do **not** equal a verified headcount.
2. **Cauldron — Oct 19, 2018.** Venue-change announcement, original project files and a post-event team retrospective support an executed event with a late move to Shilo Inn. Retrospective acknowledges low turnout and operational challenges. Artist availability worksheet is not the performed lineup.
3. **Crystallize — Dec 21, 2018.** Brown Paper Tickets day-of buyer record, organizer–venue beverage-service correspondence, and a Dec 29 firsthand attendee inquiry establish strong contemporary evidence of event activity. Emerald of Siam was identified in correspondence as planned alcohol provider; do not equate this with confirmed catering.
4. **Entombed — Feb 22, 2019.** Day-of Square summary recorded $1,323 and 71 general-ticket items, plus other categories; next-day rollover sales recorded separately. Original lineup and Brown Paper Tickets records exist. These are sales evidence, not independently verified headcount.
5. **Interstellar — Apr 26, 2019.** Day-of Square report recorded $1,420 and 74 general-ticket items, with additional items and next-day sales. Original post-event accounting and BPT records survive. **Original Interstellar After Movie.mp4** (created May 13, 2019, 77,819,707 bytes) was retrieved and visually reviewed. It shows actual event activity, stage trussing, lighting, visuals, audience and dance/performance scenes; it opens with Bonsai Events/Interstellar branding and closes with **“CHANCELOR.”** A separate Apr 29 payment to **Chancelor Perez** states **“for interstellar video”** ($200). Together these corroborate Chancelor's video-production contribution. The film does not independently authenticate attendance totals or identify all dancers.
6. **Nukid — Jun 28, 2019.** Booking/management correspondence, project folder and financial worksheet survive. Day-of Square report recorded $782 and 37 general-ticket items; May 28 $100 payment to Chancelor specifies **“Nukid promo video.”** A payment for promotional work is not by itself proof of release or full performance.
7. **Ignition — Jul 26, 2019.** Day-of Square report recorded $84 and 14 general-ticket items, with separate next-day activity. Project folder and promotional graphics survive. The data do not establish final attendance.
8. **Extinction — planned Sep 21, 2019.** Posters, volunteer schedule and Sept 3 access request exist; completed event remains **unverified**.

## Evidence and limits
- Day-of and next-day Square emails are account-wide transaction reports, not audited event settlements. Do not add channels or date windows without reconciling overlap.
- Buyer lists prove ticket transactions, not entry scans.
- Internal recap footage supports actual production activity but is not independent press verification.
- Advertisements and proposed sponsors are not proof of participation.
- Do not publish ticket buyer lists, private customer data, private email addresses, banking/payment identifiers or unverified endorsements.
- Respect user-directed exclusions from the public professional history. An unexecuted artist booking must never be represented as a completed show.
