# Independently documented community-event participation

## December 2017 — Polar Express Christmas party for foster-community children

**Source:** [When in Tri-Cities / Alicia Walters archived blog](https://aliciawaltersblog.wordpress.com/)

**Evidence:** A first-person local account of the Polar Express Christmas Party in Kennewick credits Bonsai Audio among collaborating local businesses, and explicitly reports that Brandon and a collaborator from Bonsai Audio provided Christmas music at LionsGate Chapel. The account describes children enjoying dinner and dancing. The page includes event photographs.

**Evidence level:** Independent first-person event account; supports actual involvement in a completed community celebration, not merely a planned booking. The account does not establish an amount raised, monetary value of donated services, or precise contract terms.

**Privacy:** This public research summary deliberately omits names of children and other private individuals. Do not reproduce photos of children from the event without an appropriate permission review. The collaborator's name is omitted from this summary because identifying them is not necessary to document the professional role.

**Verification follow-up:** Locate the specific permalink for the Polar Express article (rather than linking only the archive homepage) and check for other public organizer or partner acknowledgments. Do not treat the event as a fundraiser unless a source explicitly calls it one.

## Other leads from the same archived publication

The archive also reports on the May 2018 Hands In for Hands On fundraiser and March 2018 Mid-Columbia Arts Fundraiser. Those entries **do not presently credit Bonsai Audio**, so they are leads for searching organizer programs, not verified Bonsai engagements.

## Method

Separate documented participation from event-only mentions. A source that announces a fundraiser but does not name Bonsai Audio must not be presented as evidence of Bonsai's involvement.
