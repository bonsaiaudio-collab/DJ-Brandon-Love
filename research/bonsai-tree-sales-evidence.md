# Bonsai Audio — bonsai tree sales and COVID-era diversification

Research date: 2026-11-09 UTC.

## Independent public reporting
- [Tri-Cities Area Journal of Business, April 15 2021](https://www.tricitiesbusinessnews.com/articles/3191) reports the pandemic pivot from DJ/AV events to bonsai tree sales, first imported shipments, local retail/market activity, and indicative product pricing. It identifies CG Public House drive-through markets as a sales channel.
- [Tri-City Herald, July 25 2021](https://www.tri-cityherald.com/news/politics-government/election/article252924408.html) separately describes diversification into bonsai sales following pandemic event cancellations.

## Owner-held order evidence
- Connected Gmail exact-pattern search `subject:"A New Order has Arrived" "Bonsai Audio" after:2020/01/01 before:2023/01/01` yielded **46 Squarespace order notification messages**. These messages are **not all confirmed bonsai tree orders** and must be classified individually.
- Sampled Squarespace order #00110 (June 9 2021) lists a 6-inch juniper Japanese bonsai tree; order #00112 (June 23 2021) lists cherry blossom bonsai and bonsai accessories; order #00114 (Sept 11 2021) lists bonsai-related merchandise.
- Original order notifications contain purchaser identities, shipping details, itemized products and amounts. **Keep purchaser identities, shipping addresses, contact details and payment information private**. Do not publish a buyer list.
- At least one September 2021 purchaser correspondence thread concerns a requested refund following high shipping-cost discussion. **An order notification alone does not prove a completed, nonrefunded sale.**
- An email about 2020 and 2021 sales discusses tax treatment and a 2020 shipment of three bonsai trees, but should be corroborated against transaction records before inclusion in totals.

## Evidence levels and next audit
1. Categorize all 46 Squarespace order notifications by product (live tree, accessory, digital/non-tree), year, customer order, and status (ordered, fulfilled, refunded/cancelled, unknown).
2. Deduplicate order IDs and check refunds or fulfillment notices.
3. Compile aggregate number of tree orders and units sold where records permit; distinguish units from orders and buyers.
4. Correlate sales with Square receipts, drive-through markets, supply invoices, and financial records without exposing buyers.

Conclusion: the bonsai sales pivot is supported by contemporaneous independent reporting and original e-commerce order notices. Customer-level evidence exists privately, but the count of completed bonsai-tree sales is not yet established.
