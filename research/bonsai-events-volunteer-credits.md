## Attribution standards
These are **historical planning credits** rather than endorsements, testimonials, or confirmations of completed service. Some individuals may have been paid, volunteers, or contractors; the documents do not consistently establish compensation status. We use “scheduled crew” when the role is known but employment/volunteer status is not. Publicly identifying people in this historical roster should not imply their current affiliation or consent to promotional endorsements.
